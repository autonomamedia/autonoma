Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

1. Introduction

At Autonoma ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Email address, name, and contact details provided when creating an account.
  • Integration Data: OAuth tokens and profile details from third-party services like TikTok, which you explicitly authorize us to access to post content on your behalf.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our platform (e.g., features used, session duration).
  • Content Data: The videos, images, scripts, and metadata you upload or generate for publishing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Autonoma platform and its core functionalities.
  • To securely authenticate and communicate with third-party APIs (e.g., TikTok Direct Post) on your behalf.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand our services.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support.

4. Third-Party Integrations & API Usage

Autonoma connects with external services, primarily TikTok, to automate content publishing.

  • We securely store OAuth tokens using industry-standard encryption (AES-256-GCM).
  • We only access the permissions you explicitly grant during the authorization flow.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or API credentials to third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to secure your personal data from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, request deletion of, or rectify your personal data. You can always revoke Autonoma's access to your third-party accounts (like TikTok) directly from the security settings of those respective platforms.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
